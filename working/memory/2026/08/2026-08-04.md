## Decisions

- User-controllable Safe is the default; vendor operational signers are scoped
  and revocable.
- Email/social login authorizes a service signer and does not become an
  irremovable on-chain owner.
- Support both in-place vendor rotation and encrypted full export/import.
- Create `did:pkh` locally by default without public registration.
- Base Sepolia first and Scroll Sepolia only as a later validated lane.
