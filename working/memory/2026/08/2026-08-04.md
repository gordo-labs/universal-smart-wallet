## Decisions

- User-controllable Safe is the default; vendor operational signers are scoped
  and revocable.
- Email/social login authorizes a service signer and does not become an
  irremovable on-chain owner.
- Support both in-place vendor rotation and encrypted full export/import.
- Create `did:pkh` locally by default without public registration.
- Base Sepolia first and Scroll Sepolia only as a later validated lane.

## SSW-034 modular passkey authentication

- Added `@ssw/auth-passkey` with browser/server WebAuthn ports, single-use
  challenge store, origin/RP/challenge/account/DID/user-verification/code-hash
  binding, step-up verification, deterministic fixtures, and typed cancellation
  or authenticator-unavailable errors.
- Rotation verifies and persists a replacement before revoking the old signer;
  account and DID remain stable. Removal requires an active replacement.
- PRF is optional and capability-detected; the explicit passphrase fallback is
  selected when unavailable. No private key material or custom cryptography is
  implemented.
- Validation: `pnpm --filter @ssw/auth-passkey test` passes 6/6. No external
  WebAuthn, RPC, bundler, paymaster, or credential services used.
