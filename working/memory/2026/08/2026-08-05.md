## Últimas decisiones

- Support institutional and self-attested credentials with non-upgradable
  assurance labels.
- React Native + Expo for mobile; online/offline QR in v1, BLE/NFC later.
- KMS/HSM signer ports and a signed off-chain trust registry.
- Target EUDI/eIDAS interoperability without claiming certification.
- Pin each credential format/profile/version and reject implicit downgrade;
  legacy JWT-VC remains verify-only.
