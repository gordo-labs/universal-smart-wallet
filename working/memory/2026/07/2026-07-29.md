## Decisions

- Local/off-chain credential exchange is the first vertical slice.
- `is_over_18` is an issuer-signed derived claim; ZK predicates are later.
- SD-JWT VC and ERC-7579 are isolated behind pinned adapters.
- Safe is the leading smart-account candidate, subject to `SSW-015`.
- WebAuthn PRF is optional; vault recovery cannot depend on universal PRF
  support.
